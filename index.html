FAQ

Before you message us.

What does DRP9 Tech build?

DRP9 Tech builds mobile apps, web platforms and marketplaces, AI agents and automation, and runs dedicated product teams that build and operate a product with you.

Can you build an app for my project?

Yes. Tell us what you are building, the stage it is at and your timeline. We reply by email and agree scope before any work starts.

Who is behind DRP9 Tech?

DRP9 Tech is led by three co-founders: Mohamed El Sawaf (CEO), Khaled Gomaa (Chief Technology and Product Officer) and Kareem Zaher (Chief AI Officer). Between them they have led product and engineering at companies including Booking.com, BetterUp and Catawiki.

How big is the team?

More than 50 developers and account managers work with us across the world, in many industries.

Do you work with governments?

Yes. Our team has built software for government entities in Saudi Arabia and the UAE for more than ten years. The work is confidential. Read how we work with government.

Where are you based?

We work from Cairo, Amsterdam, Lisbon and Dubai.

Which companies are part of the ecosystem?

Rise Personal (personal branding, Dubai), Bnsights (innovation and government excellence consulting, Dubai), Kangaroo Parade (AI consultancy and software studio, Portugal) and KZ Advisory (advisory, Netherlands).
