Tenant, resident or super app: which fits?

Choose by the people who use the property. A tenant app serves companies in commercial buildings. A resident app serves households in a compound. A super app grows from either, once people use it every day and you add services and commerce.

Email us How we work

By the DRP9 Tech founders

The three options

Tenant app

Companies in buildings

Requests, bills, bookings, visitors and staff passes.

Resident app

Households in a compound

Gates, visitors, requests, community news and services.

Super app

Daily use at scale

Adds ordering, offers and partner services on top of the basics.

How to decide

  • Start with the group that has the most daily friction.
  • Build the habits first: access, requests, notices.
  • Add commerce and offers once people open the app every day.
