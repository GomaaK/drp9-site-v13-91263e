Where AI helps a property owner, and where it does not

AI helps most with routine, repeated work: taking requests, asking for missing details, assigning jobs and answering common questions. It does not replace judgement on disputes, safety or anything the data does not support.

Good fits

  • Receiving and routing requests.
  • Assigning and following up maintenance.
  • Answering routine questions.

Keep with people

  • Disputes and complaints that need judgement.
  • Safety decisions.
  • Anything outside the agent's scope.
