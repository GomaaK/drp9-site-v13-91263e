What is a community app for a compound or mall?

A community app is one app for everyone who uses a property: tenants, their staff, visitors and the property team. It covers getting in, parking, requests, bookings, news and payments.

Email us How we work

By the DRP9 Tech founders · Updated 5 November 2026

What it does day to day

  • Gets visitors in without a call to the front desk.
  • Lets people report a fault with a photo and see its status.
  • Shows what is on, what is open and what it costs.
  • Gives the property team one console for requests, bills and notices.

Questions to ask before you build

  • Who are the three or four user groups, and what does each do daily?
  • Which of your current systems must it connect to?
  • What should phase one cover, and what can wait?
  • Who in your team owns it after launch?

Is a community app the same as a super app?

A super app grows from a community app. It adds services and commerce once daily habits exist.

The property owner or developer, usually as a build price and then yearly maintenance.
