How a community app earns for its owner

A community app can support several income channels: parking and access services, orders on site, bookings, partner offers and advertising. The rule is to earn only after people rely on the app every day.

Email us How we work

By the DRP9 Tech founders

Channels to consider

  • Parking and access services.
  • Food, retail and service orders.
  • Room, amenity and event bookings.
  • Partner offers.
  • Advertising placements, measured by group and never by individual.

How to sequence them

  • Build the daily habits first.
  • Add the channel closest to what people already do.
  • Measure before adding the next one.

Common questions

Should we monetise from day one?

We advise against it. Habits come first, then commerce and advertising become worth selling.

Is advertising safe for privacy?

Yes, if placements are sold with consent and measured by group, never by individual.
