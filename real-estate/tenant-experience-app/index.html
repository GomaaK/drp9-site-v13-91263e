A tenant experience app for commercial and mixed-use property.

A tenant experience app gives the companies in your buildings one place to move in, ask for help, book, pay and invite visitors. It gives your team a clear view of every request and every bill.

Email us How we work

The tenant journey, from move-in to move-out

1

Move in

Handover steps, a countdown and a clear list of what is next.

2

Run the office

Requests, room bookings, deliveries and visitors.

3

Pay

One bill for the month, with spending limits for staff.

4

Change and leave

Joiners and leavers managed in the app, and a clean move-out.

What the tenant company gets

  • One place for finance, facilities and people teams to work with the property.
  • Every request tracked from report to fix, with photos and status.
  • Staff passes for access, parking and services.
  • Prices shown before anyone orders.

What your team gets

  • One console for requests, bills and notices across buildings.
  • Every request with an owner and a deadline.
  • Fewer phone calls, because the answer is already in the app.
  • Reports on what tenants ask for and what they use.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a tenant experience app?

It is an app that brings the services a tenant company uses in a building into one place: requests, bills, bookings, visitors and notices.

How is it different from property management software?

Property management software is for the owner's back office. A tenant experience app is what tenants and their staff use. The two can share data.

Can it work across several buildings?

Yes. One app and one console can cover several buildings, with each tenant seeing only theirs.
