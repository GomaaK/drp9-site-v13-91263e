Where AI helps in a property

Requests

Receive and route

The agent collects the details and photos, then sends the request to the right person.

Maintenance

Assign and follow up

The agent books the work, confirms with the contractor and closes the loop with the tenant.

Questions

Answer the routine

Opening times, how to book, where to go. The team handles the rest.

Where it does not help

  • Decisions that need judgement, such as disputes or safety calls. A person decides.
  • Anything the data does not support. If the facts are missing, the agent asks a person.
  • A messy process. We fix the process first, then automate it.

FAQ

Common questions

What can AI handle in property operations?

Routine work: taking requests, asking for missing details, assigning jobs, following up and answering common questions.

Will AI replace the facilities team?

No. It takes the repetitive part so the team can spend time on work that needs a person.

How do you keep it safe?

Agents work inside a limited scope. Anything outside it goes to a person, with the conversation attached.

What does Adam show?

Adam shows that agents can handle property service orders from start to finish. Over 80 percent of its orders run with no human intervention.
