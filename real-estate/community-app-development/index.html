Community app development for compounds, malls and districts.

A community app puts everything people do in your property in one app: getting in, parking, asking for help, booking, paying and hearing from you. We design and build it around your property, your tenants and your operations team.

Email us How we work

What a community app is

A community app is the single app a property gives to everyone who uses it: tenants, their staff, visitors and the property team.

It is not a brochure and not a portal for rent. It is the daily tool: the thing people open to get in, park, report a fault, book a room, order lunch or read a notice.

What people do in it

Arrive

Get in, park, host a guest

Visitors can receive a pass by link, with no install and no account. Hosts invite guests, with parking if needed.

Work

Get things done

Report a fault with a photo, book a room, receive a delivery, read the news from the property team.

Enjoy

The rest of the day

Find your way around, order food, use services and partner offers on site.

Two sides of one product

For the people in the property

  • One login for every role.
  • Every request shows who owns it and its status.
  • Prices are shown before you order.
  • One bill for the month for a tenant company, with spending limits for staff.

For the team that runs it

  • One console for requests, parking, bills and notices.
  • Clear owners and deadlines for every request.
  • Reports built from the same events the app records.
  • Room to add services, offers and advertising later.

How a community app can earn

Revenue is one of the things the app is built to grow, across several channels.

  • Parking and access services.
  • Orders for food, retail and services on site.
  • Bookings of rooms, amenities and events.
  • Partner offers.
  • Advertising placements, measured by group and never by individual.

How it is built

Architecture
A shared backend with an app for people, a web portal for tenant companies and a console for staff. Modules are added phase by phase.
Visitor access
Passes issued as links or QR codes, checked at the gate by plate or code.
Integrations
Gate and access systems, parking, payments, accounting and e-invoicing, WhatsApp and SMS, building systems.
AI
A concierge and request assistant that answers and routes, with people reviewing what matters.
Ownership
Source code, data and accounts are transferred to the owner.
FAQ

Common questions

What is a community app?

It is one app for everyone who uses a property: tenants, their staff, visitors and the property team. It covers access, parking, requests, bookings, news and payments.

What features should it have?

Start with what people do daily: getting in, hosting guests, reporting faults and reading notices. Add bookings, orders, bills, offers and analytics as the next phases.

Can visitors use it without installing anything?

Yes. We can design guest passes that open from a link, with no install and no account.

How is it different from a tenant portal?

A portal is usually a website for rent and documents. A community app is the daily tool for everyone in the property, including visitors.

How long does it take?

We agree dates with you after a short scoping step, before any build starts. Each phase is delivered and approved before the next begins.
