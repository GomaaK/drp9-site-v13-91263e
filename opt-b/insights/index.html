What is a community app for a compound or mall?

A community app is one app for everyone who uses a property: tenants, their staff, visitors and the property team. It covers getting in, parking, requests, bookings, news and payments.

Read more Guide

Tenant, resident or super app: which fits?

Choose by the people who use the property. A tenant app serves companies in commercial buildings. A resident app serves households in a compound. A super app grows from either, once people use it every day and you add services and commerce.

Read more Guide

How a community app earns for its owner

A community app can support several income channels: parking and access services, orders on site, bookings, partner offers and advertising. The rule is to earn only after people rely on the app every day.

Read more Guide

Build or buy a community app: how to decide

Buy when your needs match a standard product and you accept its roadmap. Build when your property, your tenants and your operations are specific, and you want to own the product and its data.

Delivering a community app in phases

A phased delivery builds the app in steps. Before each phase is built, you see and approve a clickable demo. When it is delivered, you test it and sign off before the next phase begins.

Where AI helps a property owner, and where it does not

AI helps most with routine, repeated work: taking requests, asking for missing details, assigning jobs and answering common questions. It does not replace judgement on disputes, safety or anything the data does not support.

Launching a marketplace app: the decisions that matter

A marketplace works when sellers have a reason to join, buyers trust the transaction, and your team can run the operations. Decide those three things before you decide features.

AI agents for customer calls: what to automate first

Start with calls that are frequent, clear and low risk. Let the agent handle them end to end, and hand anything unusual to a person with the full notes.
