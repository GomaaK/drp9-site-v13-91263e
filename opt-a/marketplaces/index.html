FAQ

Common questions

What does a marketplace need to launch?

A clear group of sellers, a reason for buyers to come, a way to pay and a way to handle problems. The software follows from those.

Do you build both the app and the operations tools?

Yes. The app buyers use and the tools your team uses to run it.

Do you work with early startups and large companies?

Both. The founders have led products at startups and at companies such as Booking.com, BetterUp and Catawiki.
