FAQ

Common questions

What is an AI agent?

It is software that takes a task, uses tools such as your systems and messages, and completes it, asking a person when it is unsure.

What should we automate first?

A routine task that is frequent, clear and costs you time. See our guide on customer calls.

Is it safe to put AI in front of customers?

Yes, if it works inside a limited scope, a person takes the exceptions, and the results are tested before release.
