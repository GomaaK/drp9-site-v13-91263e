FAQ

Common questions

What is real estate software?

It is software that helps owners and operators run property: how people get in, ask for help, pay, book and hear from you. Community apps and tenant apps are the part people use every day. Operations tools are the part your team uses.

Who is a community app for?

Developers and owners of compounds, malls, business parks and mixed-use districts who want one place for tenants, visitors and staff.

Does it replace our property management system?

No. It sits beside your existing systems and connects to the ones that matter. We agree which ones during the first stage.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes. We work with owners and operators in Egypt, the Gulf and Europe, from teams in Cairo, Dubai, Lisbon and Amsterdam.
